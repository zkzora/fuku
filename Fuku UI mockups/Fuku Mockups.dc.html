AI agents are about to move money. Nobody knows who's behind them.

No reliable identity for the human behind an agent. No portable reputation. No universal permission framework. Every application rebuilds its own trust system. Fuku is the primitive they build on instead.

Human Identity

Passkey sign‑in with WebAuthn. One verified human, provably behind every agent they own.

Permission Passport

What is this AI allowed to do? Allowances, limits and hard blocks, enforced on‑chain, readable by anyone.

96/100
Portable Reputation

A trust score built from execution history, violations and security events. It travels with the agent.

Why this scoreExplained by Kimi

Trading Assistant is owned by a verified human and has completed 1,284 approved swaps within its daily limit over 30 days. The score would be 99, but one blocked withdrawal attempt this morning cost 3 points. Because the attempt was stopped by policy and the agent stayed within limits afterwards, the penalty decays over 14 days if no further violations occur.
